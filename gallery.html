The Gallery

A look inside the space

Interwoven Maker Space

A venue created by an artist for artists, it becomes a space to make creativity blossom. It is a gallery for artists, a performance space for musicians and poets, a place to teach a class and learn new skills - a space where everyone is welcome to create and engage with local artists and their art.

Have some tea or coffee over conversation, all together it forms the culture, art, food, fabric and entertainment of the community.

This vision of Interwoven Caffe™ is to be open for events, classes, and scheduled city events.

Music & Events

Interwoven is a venue for many performers, including those that are a part of Stoveboat Concerts and Unexpected Music.

Food

As someone of Sicilian heritage, making food is a process Rhonda feels deeply connected to. Like many of the cultures that live in the United States, we all come from somewhere. The way we make our food and the ingredients we use reflect the origins of our cultural heritage. In the Art and Language of Food, Rhonda teaches cooking and food preservation and give our Grandmothers all the credit. You can also find her at the New Bedford Farmer's market sharing food with The Coastal Foodshed.
