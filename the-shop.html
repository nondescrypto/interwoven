Support the Space

Dyer Maker Studio's home base is located at Interwoven, a venue created by the Artist Rhonda M. Fazio for Artists. A safe haven for Artists in gentrified cities to afford a space while surviving to thrive as we continue the struggle to support ourselves through a "gig" economy. Interwoven is an independent entity grateful for any contributions that assist the venue's mission to remain open in an ever changing landscape of former industrial economies, always in support of the independent Artists' pursuit and determination to succeed.
