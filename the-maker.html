The Maker

The hands behind the work

Rhonda M. Fazio

Environmental Alchemist: Food & Fabric

Rhonda M. Fazio is the founder and designer of Dyer Maker Studio, a traveling Maker Space specializing in natural colors and sustainable textile design.

Rhonda teaches "Dyeing to Wear It", weaving and cooking classes and is the Creative Curator of Interwoven. She is a caterer for events, known as La Rhonda.

"My work with food and fabric reflect the essential connection between people from all over the world in relation to the journey of the human narrative. In America, it is a story we all share, and we are all Interwoven because of it."

Makers Collaborating

Kim Gatesman

Painting + Printmaking + Tinkering

Kim is a painter, printmaker, and an overall creative with a professional career that spans a range of disciplines and industries over that last 20 years. With numerous bodies of work, Kim covers a variety of genres from realism to abstract, colorful to black and white, and traditional to esoteric, with all showing the same level of skill and dedication across her portfolio.

"I care as much about how work is made as why it’s made, therefore the practical foundation for all of my work is a combination of technique, craftsmanship, intent, and process, all of which give my interests something stable on which to grow. However, the primary drive behind why I make art is the desire for a genuinely honest and thoughtful means of expression, full of fascination and wonder. Unsurprisingly then, my work represents the wide variety of subjects and processes that pique my interests and the quirky ways said variety combines in my imagination. "
