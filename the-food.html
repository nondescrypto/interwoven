Seafood at Sunset

Sundays up on a roof or on a deck

Each Sunday throughout the summer, La Rhonda features what's in season in and out of the water (and in a tin), highlighting the city's beautiful harbour through the art of fresh, tapas-style seafood dishes. Come on up and enjoy the vibe and the food — it's free.

The Button Flower

The "Button Flower" is designed as a keepsake to pay tribute to the women, children and men who worked in the textile making industries and mills for centuries. With no name to their faces, and no way to ever sign their work, some paid the ultimate price with their lives, their dignity, and the skills never to be recognized and compensated for their worth - discarded and forgotten - like a button.

The constant bloom of the flower secured with vintage buttons from the Artist's collection, ensures the memory of someone you knew or never met will not wither away despite never being seen or heard.
